PIER accessibility statement

This statement applies to the beta version of PIER, version 53.

Accessibility status

We are actively improving PIER against WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We do not yet claim conformance because the generated payroll PDF is not tagged or fully selectable, and testing with the full range of assistive technologies is not complete.

What has been improved

  • Keyboard-operable tabs, visible focus, a skip link and labelled dialogs.
  • Readable journey editing separate from the payroll-document preview.
  • Persistent form errors, deletion Undo actions and explicit shift-review states.
  • Responsive layouts, larger controls, reduced-motion support and display preferences.

Known limitations

  • The official payroll PDF output is canvas-based and does not yet have document tags or a verified screen-reader reading order.
  • A typed or uploaded signature alternative requires payroll approval and is not currently available.
  • NVDA, VoiceOver and TalkBack testing remains to be completed on supported physical devices.

Feedback

Use PIER’s “Report a bug / give feedback” action to tell us about an accessibility problem. Do not include personal claim or rota information.
