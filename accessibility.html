Accessibility Status

PIER is actively being improved to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards. Full conformance is not yet claimed because the generated payroll PDF is not tagged or fully selectable, and testing across the full range of assistive technologies has not been carried out.

What Has Been Improved

  • Keyboard-operable tabs, visible focus indicators, a skip link, and labelled dialogs.
  • Readable journey editing separate from your payroll-document preview.
  • Persistent form errors, deletion Undo actions, and explicit shift-review states.
  • Responsive layouts, larger controls, reduced-motion support, and display preferences.

Known Limitations

  • The official payroll PDF output is canvas-based and does not yet have document tags or a verified screen-reader reading order.
  • A typed signature alternative requires payroll approval and is not currently available.
  • Full testing with NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack remains to be completed on supported physical devices.
