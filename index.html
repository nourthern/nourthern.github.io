How PIER Handles Your Data

Your claim data stays safely stored on your device. PIER only collects a minimal amount of anonymous information to keep the app running smoothly and support ongoing improvements.

What Is Collected

  • App Usage: A randomly generated ID code, your app version, the date you last used the app, and basic counts of actions you take (like creating PDFs, setting up notifications, or backing up data).
  • Group Stats: Overall claim totals for the past 3 and 12 months. This is combined with all other users' data to show general usage stats—never your personal numbers.
  • Notification Details: If you turn on push notifications, PIER stores the technical information required to deliver them.

What Stays Stored Only on Your Device

  • Your Personal Data: Your form entries, imported calendar events, signatures, claim rows, and expense logs are saved strictly in your web browser.
  • No Ad Tracking: PIER does not use advertising, tracking cookies, or device fingerprinting. Only standard browser storage is used to keep the app working offline and load your preferences.
  • Important Note: If you clear your browser history or cache, your saved data will be deleted. Always save a backup copy first using the save icon!

How Live Calendars Work

When you sync a live calendar, the system briefly uses your calendar link to fetch your schedule. PIER never saves the link or the contents of your calendar.

What Is NEVER Collected

  • Your name, address, postcode, or payroll/registration numbers.
  • Shift details, work locations, dates, or times.
  • Claim entries, mileage, miscellaneous costs, or history.
  • Form contents, exported files, or clipboard text.
  • Screenshots (unless you manually attach one to a bug report).

Feedback and Bug Reports

  • Reports are only created if you manually send feedback or report an issue.
  • You can review and remove technical details and screenshots before sending them.
  • Reports are kept for 90 days to help resolve bugs, then automatically deleted.
  • You can turn off basic usage tracking at any time using the setting below. Push notifications can be turned off separately.

Help & tutorial

  1. Setup: connect a calendar and enter the details used on the claim form. Add a sample signature and optionally configure reminders.
  2. Shifts: choose one or more claim months, then check every imported shift. Study-related events are highlighted and default to “Do not claim”.
  3. Claim form: review each document preview. Tap or click a preview, or use Edit table, to change individual cells. Then sign and save the selected PDFs. Use the Email payroll button to generate a pre-drafted email. Don’t forget to attach the generated form(s) and any relevant receipts!
  4. Expense log: use the local log to track submitted claims. Delete an entry if it should no longer be included in the cumulative total.

Backups: use the save icon to create a backup of Setup and Log tabs. This is particularly important before clearing browser data or changing device.

This tool assists with form preparation only. You remain responsible for checking eligibility and accuracy against Trust and payroll rules.

About PIER

PIER is a travel claims manager, designed to streamline the process of creating forms for submission to payroll. A true Painless and Intelligent Expenses Reporting tool! All the magic happens locally so claim details remain on your device. The site functions as a webapp that can be saved to your phone's homescreen or desktop.

Built over a few late nights to solve a personal headache, I hope PIER makes life easier for everyone in the same boat and puts some money back in your pocket!
