Privacy and usage data

Travel Claims Manager stores your claim information locally on this device. A small amount of privacy-preserving usage data is sent to help improve the tool.

What is collected

  • A random installation ID and the date the app was last used
  • App version and broad feature-use counts, such as calendar imports, PDFs created, backups and notification setup
  • Your current cumulative total claimed in the last three months, sent only as a single total and used for an all-users total
  • Optional push-notification subscription details and coarse monthly status flags when push is enabled
This app never collects
  • Names, addresses, postcodes or personal numbers
  • Registration numbers
  • Calendar URLs, tokens or login details
  • Shift names, dates, times or locations
  • Claim rows, mileage, toll costs or individual financial totals
  • Form contents or exported documents
  • Clipboard contents
  • Persistent device fingerprints
  • Screenshots unless you deliberately attach one after checking it
Additional data gathered for diagnostics
  • Generated only when something fails or you open Report a bug
  • Shown to you in Review technical details before submission
  • Removable by you before submission; the technical details themselves are read-only
  • Retained briefly, for 90 days
  • Linked using a one-off report ID, not your installation ID

You can turn ordinary usage telemetry off below. Push notifications are controlled separately.

Help & tutorial

  1. Setup: connect your calendar and enter the details used on your claim form. Add a sample signature and optionally configure reminders.
  2. Shifts: choose one or more claim months, then check every imported shift. Study-related events appear in orange and default to “Do not claim”.
  3. Claim form: review each document preview. Tap or click a preview, or use Edit table, to change individual cells. Then sign and save the selected PDFs.
  4. Expense log: use the local log to track submitted claims. Delete an entry if it should no longer be included in your cumulative total.

Backups: use Backup site data regularly, particularly before clearing browser data or changing device.

This tool assists with form preparation only. You remain responsible for checking eligibility and accuracy against Trust and payroll rules.
